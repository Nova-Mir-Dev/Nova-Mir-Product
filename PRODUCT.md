# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**Admins** — small business owners or agency operators managing client websites, leads, projects, billing, and content. They are busy, often non-technical, and need a dashboard that gives them confidence at a glance. Primary workflow: triage leads → manage projects → invoice clients.

**Clients** — business owners who have purchased a website or operations system. They need to view project progress, pay invoices, upload documents, and contact support. They should feel taken care of, not lost in a corporate portal.

**Public visitors** — potential clients evaluating Nova Mir. They need to understand the service tiers, see portfolio work, and get a sense of the brand's personality before reaching out.

## Product Purpose

Nova Mir builds custom websites and operations systems for service businesses. The product is both the service itself and the software platform that delivers it — the admin dashboard runs the business, the client portal delivers the experience, and the marketing site sells it.

Success looks like: an admin who can manage their entire client workflow without leaving the dashboard, a client who always knows where their project stands, and a public site that converts visitors with clarity and warmth.

## Brand Personality

**Warm, Approachable, Capable.** Not sterile corporate SaaS, not over-designed startup fluff. Like a real person who knows what they're doing built this for you.

Voice is direct but friendly — confident without arrogance, helpful without hand-holding. Copy avoids jargon and speaks to the business owner, not a developer.

## Anti-references

- **AI chatbot wrapper** — the standard left-chat-right-panel layout. Nova Mir is a tool, not a conversation.
- **No-code builder UI** — cluttered panels with drag handles everywhere. Not a builder, a management tool.
- **Generic SaaS admin** — Bootstrap/AdminLTE-style dashboards with heavy sidebars and dated gradients. Should feel bespoke, not templated.
- **Corporate enterprise tool** — SAP/Salesforce-style dense tables, gray-on-gray. Should feel human, not institutional.
- **SaaS cliché hero-metric template** — big number, small label, gradient accent, supporting stats. Dashboards earn their metrics through context, not decoration.

## Design Principles

1. **Confidence through clarity** — Every page should answer "What do I do here?" in under 2 seconds. Use heading hierarchy, visual weight, and whitespace to guide attention. An admin should never wonder what to do next.

2. **Bespoke, not templated** — Nothing should look like it came from a theme. Azimuth provides the foundation, but the assembly and rhythm should feel custom to Nova Mir. Avoid stock layouts.

3. **Human-first admin** — Admin tools default to dense and cold. Push for warmth: rounded corners, generous spacing, thoughtful microcopy. Even a data table can feel approachable with the right whitespace and tone.

4. **Empty states are opportunities** — Every empty table, fresh install, or first-time view should guide the user forward, not show a blank page. Use empty states to teach, not just to say "nothing here."

5. **AAA accessibility is the target** — Not just compliance. Higher contrast ratios, generous touch targets, clear focus indicators, and semantic HTML throughout. If a choice between aesthetic and accessible comes up, accessible wins.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA as baseline, AAA where practical
- Higher contrast ratios on all text and UI elements
- Screen reader testing priority — semantic HTML, ARIA labels, live regions for dynamic content
- Encourage prefers-reduced-motion support
- Color is never the sole indicator of meaning
